# Malaysia National Physics Competition / Physics Olympiad selection — source links

Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia's Physics Olympiad site preserves official Malaysian national-selection material. A complete **2007 National Physics Competition** is directly verified for both **Secondary** and **Pre-University** levels, with an official Secondary answer key and a Pre-University question/marking-scheme file. The UKM Physics Olympiad programme remains the institutional selection/training source for Malaysia.
