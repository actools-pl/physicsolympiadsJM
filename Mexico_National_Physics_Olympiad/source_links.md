# Mexico National Physics Olympiad — direct exam and solution links

The Mexican Physical Society (SMF) official archive exposes national-Olympiad files from the 1990s through 2024. **Theory + experimental problem/solution pairs are present from 2011/2012 through 2024** (some organizer rows use the same experimental file for the statement and solution). Earlier rows mostly expose theory papers only. The exact organizer file targets are recorded below.

Official archive: https://smf.mx/programas/olimpiada-nacional-de-fisica/examenes/
