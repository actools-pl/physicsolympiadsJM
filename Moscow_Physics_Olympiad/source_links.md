# Moscow Physics Olympiad — source links

The official Moscow Physics Olympiad site maintains a **past-problem archive** and historical notes. The archive contains past-year problem statements and answer/solution material by grade and round. The organizers also document the published solved collection covering **1986–2005**, with 2006–2007 appended.
