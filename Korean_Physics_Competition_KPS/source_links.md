# Korean Physical Society Physics Competition / Physics League — source links

The Korean Physical Society (KPS) currently publishes official **Physics Competition (The Physics League)** past papers and answer sheets. The visible official board contains both annual sittings for **2025 and 2026**, plus 2024 material, with separate elementary/intermediate/advanced/expert-level papers and answers.

This modern KPS competition should be kept distinct from the historical Korean Physics Olympiad (KPhO) selection system.
